CAD Modeler: feature tour

A Fusion 360-style parametric modeler (Electron, three.js, OpenCascade) with in-app CFD and FEA, generative design and a gear wizard. Every screenshot below was captured from the running app by tools/feature-tour.cjs.

1Constrained sketching

Sketches on planes or faces with lines, rectangles, circles, arcs and polygons, driven by a constraint solver. Sketch Dimension (D) infers lengths, distances, angles, diameters and radii; constraint glyphs are selectable, fully constrained geometry turns black, and the Sketch Palette shows the remaining degrees of freedom.

2Extrude with live preview

Extrude, Revolve and Press Pull pick sketch profiles or planar faces. One side, symmetric or two sides; Join, Cut, Intersect or New Body. The preview updates live, with a drag arrow and an in-canvas value box.

3Fillet, chamfer and shell

Fillet, Chamfer and Shell work on the real B-rep (OpenCascade), with live previews. Every feature lands on the parametric timeline: double-click to edit, drag the marker to roll back, and failed features turn red with the reason.

4ISO fasteners with modeled threads

M2–M24 and UNC bolts, cap screws, button and countersunk heads, set screws, threaded rod, nuts and washers. Click a hole rim and the part seats in it, sized automatically; threads are real helical geometry or cosmetic.

5Materials and appearance

21 engineering materials with render settings and real physical properties (density, Young's modulus, yield strength, thermal data) used by the simulations. Any colour on any material with a polished, satin or matte finish; studio lighting, ground shadow and ambient occlusion.

6Measure

INSPECT → Measure (I): face areas, edge lengths and the minimum distance between any two faces or edges.

7Command search and shortcuts

Press S anywhere to search every command by name; single-key shortcuts (E extrude, Q press pull, F fillet, J joint, K shop stock, G gears…) work as in Fusion.

8Right-click marking menu

Right-click in the canvas for Fusion's radial marking menu: repeat the last command, press pull, sketch, extrude, fillet, undo / redo, plus an overflow menu.

9Import and export

Import STEP, STL and OBJ (meshes become real solids) by menu or drag-and-drop. Export STEP, STL, OBJ, GLB (with materials) or the design file.

10Shop Stock library

About 440 things you buy rather than design: T-slot extrusion, pipe, tube, angle and channel, bar and plate, bearings, shafts, steppers and servos, and all the fasteners. Search, pick a size and length, place it on a face or in a hole; it arrives with the right material.

11Gear Wizard

Involute spur, helical, internal ring and rack gears: pairs, trains, ring + pinion, rack + pinion, planetary sets, or a new gear meshed onto one already in the design. Teeth are phased so meshing gears never overlap; the panel shows diameters, centre distances, contact ratio, ratio and output speed.

12Gears that move: joints and motion links

With “Make it move”, each gear gets a component and an axle joint on a Gear Frame, and meshing gears are tied by motion links. Drive Joints turns the sun and the planets, carrier and ring follow; links show up in the browser and export to URDF as mimic joints.

13Motion Link

ASSEMBLE → Motion Link ties any two moving joints by tooth counts or a ratio, including rotation driving a slider (rack and pinion). Links chain into trains and are checked for loops.

14Robot export for Isaac Sim / Isaac Lab

Turns the assembly into a robot package: robot.usda (UsdPhysics articulation) and robot.urdf with meshes, mass and inertia from the exact geometry and materials, drives, limits and gear mimic joints.

15Sprockets, roller chain, pulleys and timing belts

Chain drive and belt drive modes in the Gear Wizard: ANSI / ISO / bicycle roller chain on real sprocket tooth forms, GT2 / HTD timing belts on grooved pulleys. The centre distance is solved so the chain is a whole number of links (or the belt whole teeth); chaining onto a wheel that already carries a chain adds a twin sprocket on its shaft. Driving a sprocket carries the chain and belt round with it.

16NACA airfoil sketches

CREATE → NACA Airfoil: 4- and 5-digit sections from a code, chord and angle of attack, as a sketch on any plane or added to the open sketch, with a live preview and the section's thickness and camber.

172D Flow (GPU lattice Boltzmann)

A quick 2D CFD of any closed sketch on the GPU: speed, vorticity or pressure with streamlines, live lift and drag coefficients and forces per metre of span, and an inviscid panel-method lift alongside.

183D CFD inside the app

SIMULATE → CFD Study runs the GPU CFD Sim solver in the background and draws the results in the viewport: surface pressure, vortex surfaces, streamlines, slices, legends and a frame player, with drag, lift and side force.

19Static stress (FEA) inside the app

SIMULATE → Static Stress: pick fixed faces and load faces, forces or pressure and gravity; FEA Sim meshes the exact geometry into quadratic tetrahedra and solves on the GPU. Results: deformed shape, safety factor, von Mises, displacement and principal stresses with min / max probes.

20Shape Optimization (generative design)

Pick the faces that hold a part and the faces that carry loads, then ask for the lightest part that meets a safety factor (or a target mass). The body is voxelised and material is shaved away live over a ghost of the original; Create Body puts the result in the timeline.

21Under-construction tape

Every control that's there for the Fusion look but isn't built yet carries black-and-yellow tape: toolbar icons, menu items, the other tabs, and the Data Panel, notifications, comments and viewports buttons.

22Bug reports

The Bug report button saves your memo with the last five minutes of clicks and keys, a screenshot, the design and the app state into bug-reports/, ready for the next session to pick up.

23Dark theme

Light and dark themes (avatar menu or Display Settings), perspective / orthographic / perspective-with-ortho-faces cameras, ViewCube, and shaded / shaded-with-edges / wireframe styles.
